## MIDAS

The [Michigan Institute for Data and AI in Society](https://midas.umich.edu) supports AI and data science research across U-M.

- **MIDAS Model Zoo** ([GitHub](https://github.com/UM-MIDAS/model-zoo)): the catalog of AI tools, models, datasets, tutorials, and web applications described in Chapter 2. Contact the MIDAS team to contribute an entry.
- **Workshops, tutorials, and consultations** on applying AI in research, including reproducibility and sharing.
- **[MIDAS AI in Research Handbook](https://midas-ai-in-research.readthedocs.io/en/main/)**: the companion handbook covering AI across the research lifecycle.

## U-M Library

- **[Deep Blue Data](https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/data)**: U-M's curated research data repository, free to deposit for U-M researchers, with DOIs and long-term preservation (Chapter 16).
- **Research data services**: consultations on data management plans, choosing repositories, documentation, and data curation (Chapters 12 and 17).
- **Copyright Office**: guidance on copyright, fair use, and licensing for data, code, and publications (Chapter 18).

## ICPSR

The [Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu), based at U-M, curates and preserves social and behavioral science data, including restricted-use data and a Virtual Data Enclave for sensitive data (Chapters 16 and 19). ICPSR staff can advise on consent language, disclosure review, and deposit.

## Advanced Research Computing (ARC)

[ARC](https://its.umich.edu/advanced-research-computing), part of U-M Information and Technology Services, provides computing and storage for research.

- **Great Lakes**: U-M's main HPC cluster, including GPU partitions.
- **Armis2**: HPC cluster for sensitive data, including data subject to HIPAA (Chapter 19).
- **Lighthouse**: for researchers who purchase their own dedicated hardware.
- **Turbo** (active storage) and **Data Den** (archive storage).
- **Containers**: Apptainer/Singularity is available by default on ARC clusters; Docker is not (Chapter 6). See the ARC containers documentation.
- **U-M Research Computing Package (UMRCP)**: no-cost allocations for eligible researchers, including up to 80,000 CPU hours on Great Lakes and/or Armis2, up to 10 TB of Turbo storage, and up to 100 TB of Data Den storage (Chapter 21).

## Sensitive Data and Research Compliance

- **Sensitive Data Guide** (U-M Safe Computing): lists which IT services are approved for which types of sensitive data. Check it before storing or processing sensitive data anywhere (Chapter 19).
- **Institutional Review Boards** (IRBMED for medical research; IRB-HSBS for health sciences and behavioral sciences): review of human subjects research, including consent language for data sharing.
- **Office of Research and Sponsored Projects (ORSP)**: sponsor requirements, including data management and sharing plans and public access policies (Chapter 12).

## Innovation Partnerships

[U-M Innovation Partnerships](https://innovationpartnerships.umich.edu) handles intellectual property, technology transfer, and questions about open source licensing of software created at U-M (Chapter 18). Contact them before releasing code that may have commercial potential or was developed under an industry agreement.

## Digital Accessibility

U-M's digital accessibility resources provide guidance, training, and tools for making web content, documents, and media accessible, including in relation to the ADA Title II requirements discussed in Chapter 23. Your school or college may also have its own accessibility staff.

## GitHub at U-M

U-M provides access to GitHub through institutional arrangements managed by ITS, and students and educators can also receive free benefits through GitHub Education. Consider creating a lab or center organization so that shared repositories are not tied to one person's account (Chapter 20).
